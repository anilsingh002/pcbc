Clinical Precision

Specialized Component Banking & Quality Standards

Modern blood banking is component banking. One donation of 350ml or 450ml is fractionated into life-saving therapeutic fractions to treat up to three distinct patients.

Red Cells

Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC)

High hematocrit concentrate designed for acute surgical hemorrhage, severe anemia, obstetrics emergencies, and road trauma without fluid overload.

  • Stored at precise 2°C to 6°C
  • Up to 42 days viability with SAGM
  • Gel card crossmatch verified
Platelet Therapy

Single Donor Platelets (SDP / Apheresis)

Direct cell separator apheresis yields a massive therapeutic dose (≥3 × 10¹¹ platelets) from a single donor, crucial for dengue, chemotherapy, and leukemia care.

  • Continuous agitation at 20°C–24°C
  • Minimizes HLA alloimmunization risk
  • Equivalent to 6–8 random units
Plasma & Clotting

Fresh Frozen Plasma & Cryoprecipitate

Rich in all labile coagulation factors (Factor VIII, Fibrinogen, von Willebrand). Indispensable for severe burn victims, coagulopathy, and liver transplantation.

  • Flash frozen within 6 hours of draw
  • Deep frozen at -40°C to -80°C
  • Cryoprecipitate for Hemophilia A

Medical Advice

Frequently Asked Questions

Doctor-verified answers to common queries regarding voluntary blood donation and hospital requisition.

No, not at all. An adult human body holds approximately 5 to 6 litres of blood. A standard donation takes only 350ml or 450ml (under 8% of your total blood volume). Your body replaces the fluid volume within 24 to 48 hours, while new healthy red blood cells are actively generated by your bone marrow in a few weeks. Donating blood actually stimulates fresh cell production!

To requisition blood from our Tagore Town centre, please bring: (1) Official Hospital Blood Requisition Form signed and stamped by the attending doctor, (2) Appropriately labeled patient blood sample vial in an EDTA tube for cross-matching, (3) Identity card of the patient/attendant. For urgent trauma, hospitals can place advance reservations via telephone at 0532-3552983.

Apheresis is a specialized, automated process where only platelets are separated from the donor's blood using a sterile closed circuit, and all other components (red cells and plasma) are safely returned to the donor. One SDP donation yields as many platelets as 6 to 8 individual whole blood donations, offering life-saving support for cancer and dengue patients.

We follow a strict 5-tier safety workflow: (1) Rigorous medical pre-screening of donors, (2) Single-use, disposable sterile phlebotomy sets, (3) Automated Chemiluminescence / NAT testing for HIV 1&2, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, and Malaria, (4) Gel card crossmatching and Rh phenotyping, and (5) Helmer automated digital cold chain storage with round-the-clock temperature validation.
